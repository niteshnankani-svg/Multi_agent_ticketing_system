# Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method only.

To be eligible for a refund:
- The request must be made within 30 days of purchase.
- The product or service must not have been fully consumed.

Partial refunds may be issued for annual subscriptions cancelled
mid-term, calculated on a pro-rated basis.

Refunds for failed or duplicate charges are processed automatically
within 2 business days once identified.

If a refund has not appeared after 10 business days, the customer
should contact their bank, as processing times vary by financial institution.
