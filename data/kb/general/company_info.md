# General Company Information

## Business Hours
Support is available Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM IST.
Email support is monitored 24/7 with a response target of 24 hours.
We do not offer live phone support on weekends.

## Contact Channels
General inquiries: support@company.com
Sales questions: sales@company.com
Partnership inquiries: partners@company.com

## Product Overview
We offer a SaaS platform for customer support automation, including
ticket classification, automated responses, and human escalation.
Plans are available for small teams up to enterprise organizations.

## Getting a Demo
Product demos can be scheduled through our website's "Book a Demo"
button, or by emailing sales@company.com. Demos typically last 30 minutes.

## Company Background
We are a technology company founded to help support teams handle
ticket volume more efficiently using AI-assisted tools.

## Mobile App
We do not currently offer a dedicated mobile app. Our platform is
fully accessible through any mobile web browser.

## Training & Onboarding
New customers receive a guided onboarding session within their first
week. Additional training sessions can be requested through your
account manager.
